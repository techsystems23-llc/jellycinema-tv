# Jelly Cinema TV — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

Jelly Cinema TV is an app for watching your own media from a Jellyfin server on your TV. It is built so
that your viewing stays between your TV and your own server.

## What the app sends, and where

- **Your Jellyfin server.** Everything the app shows comes from the Jellyfin server you sign in to: your
  libraries, what you've watched, your favorites. The app sends the server what any Jellyfin app sends:
  your sign-in, what you play and how far you got, and the TV's name and model so the server can list
  it under Devices. Whoever runs that server can see this, as with any Jellyfin app.
- **YouTube, for trailers.** When the banner plays a title's trailer, it uses YouTube's official
  embedded player, so YouTube (Google) receives the request for that video and the TV's network
  address. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to that. Trailer files
  stored on your own server don't use YouTube. You can turn banner trailers off in
  Settings → Jelly Cinema.
- **Crash reports, only if you agree.** If the app crashes, it asks whether to send a report. If you
  choose Send report, technical details (what went wrong, the app version and the TV model) go to your
  own Jellyfin server, never to us or anyone else. No passwords are included. You can turn crash reports
  off in Settings → Crash reporting.
- **Updates.** The version that updates itself checks the app's release page, or your server, for a
  newer version. Only the request for that file is sent.
- **Google TV / Android TV home screen.** If your TV supports it, the app puts rows such as
  Continue watching on the TV's home screen. That information stays on the TV.

## What the app doesn't do

- No advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- No account with us: you only sign in to your own Jellyfin server.
- We don't receive, store or sell any of your information.

## Children

Accounts with a rating limit on the Jellyfin server only see titles within that limit, everywhere in the
app, and get Kids' Home (bigger posters, no trailers) automatically. A profile can be protected with a PIN
on the TV (Settings → Jelly Cinema → Profile PIN).
