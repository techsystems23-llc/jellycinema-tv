Jelly Cinema turns your own Jellyfin server into a living-room cinema.

• A big banner with each title's logo, rating and story, and its trailer playing quietly behind it
• Theater Mode: leave the remote alone and the banner fills the screen
• Top 10 Movies and Shows This Week, ranked from what your household actually watches
• Holiday rows that appear by themselves: Halloween, Christmas, Easter, plus falling leaves, snow and more
• Because You Watched, genre rows, Collections and Watch Again, made from your own viewing
• Weekend Picks: well-rated family titles nobody has watched yet
• Kids' Home for accounts with a rating limit: bigger posters, no trailers, nothing above the limit
• Profile PINs, so kids can't switch to a parent's profile
• Settings for everything, right on the TV

Your server, your rules: every row only shows what the signed-in person is allowed to watch, checked by an
automatic privacy test on every version. No ads, no tracking, no account with us.

Free, with nothing locked. If you enjoy it, you can leave an optional tip in Settings → Support the
developer. Also free on GitHub.

Requires a Jellyfin server (free, https://jellyfin.org) with your own media. Works with or without the
Jelly Cinema plugin for Jellyfin.
